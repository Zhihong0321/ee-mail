EE-Mail · HR QUICK GUIDE 01 / 03
New feature · simple explanation

Automated job applications

When a candidate emails vacancy@eternalgy.me, EE-Mail can read the message, save the application, reply to the candidate, and alert the correct Head of Department (HOD) on WhatsApp.

What HR needs to remember

The system works automatically. HR mainly needs to keep the HOD WhatsApp numbers up to date and check the dashboard when needed.

The four-step journey
1

A candidate sends an email

They send their CV or job enquiry to vacancy@eternalgy.me.

2

The system reads the email

It checks whether the message looks like a job application and identifies the likely department.

3

The candidate receives a reply

For a clear application, an automatic reply confirms receipt and asks for a WhatsApp number and possible interview times.

4

The right HOD is notified

The system finds the department's saved WhatsApp number and sends the HOD a short summary.

There are three possible results

✓ Job application

The application is saved, the candidate gets an automatic reply, and the matching HOD receives WhatsApp.

? Unclear message

The candidate receives a request to clarify the position or department. No HOD message is sent yet.

— Not a job application

The message is saved for record keeping and marked as ignored. No reply is sent.

How the system finds the right HOD

The system matches the department in the application with a department listed in HOD WhatsApp Settings. The matching department's saved number is then used for the WhatsApp alert.

How to add or update a HOD number

  1. Open the dashboard: ee-mail-production.up.railway.app
  2. Click HOD WhatsApp Settings in the left sidebar.
  3. Enter the department name, for example Engineering.
  4. Enter the HOD WhatsApp number with country code, for example 60123456789.
  5. Click Save mapping. The list below the form shows the saved number and status.

Important — add a “default” mapping.

If the system cannot identify a specific department, it tries the department named default (or general). Adding a default number gives unmatched applications a safe destination.

EE-Mail · DAILY HR USE 03 / 03
Check, understand, act

Where to check and what to do

Find the email in the dashboard

Open https://ee-mail-production.up.railway.app/, then open the Received Inbox tab. Click an email to read its details. Job applications are saved there even if the message needs clarification or a HOD number is missing.

Quick troubleshooting

What you seeWhat it usually meansWhat to do
No reply to the candidate The email may be marked “not a job application” or “unclear”. Check the Received Inbox. An unclear message will have a clarification reply.
HOD did not receive WhatsApp No number is saved for the matched department. Open HOD WhatsApp Settings and add or correct the department mapping.
notification_error The department or fallback number is missing. Add the matching department, or add a default mapping.

Simple checklist for HR

✓

Every active department has the correct HOD WhatsApp number.

✓

A default fallback number is configured.

✓

New applications can be found in the Received Inbox.

Privacy reminder.

Applicant names, email addresses, phone numbers and CV details are personal information. Keep the dashboard link and HOD contact list within the authorised HR team, and handle the information according to company policy.

In one sentence

Candidates email the vacancy address; EE-Mail sorts the message, replies to the candidate, saves the details, and alerts the right HOD by WhatsApp.

If a notification is missing, first check the department mapping and the default fallback.
